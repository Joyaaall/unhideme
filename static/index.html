What counts as a trace?+

unhideme searches publicly indexed Reddit pages for a username. Results may include authored posts, mentions by other people, or pages where ownership is unclear. Open the evidence on any result to see why it was classified.

Why might something be missing?+

Search indexes are incomplete and can be out of date. Deleted, private, new, or unindexed posts may not appear. No results does not mean no posts exist. This is not an archive or a direct Reddit account-history export.

What can I keep?+

Export JSON saves the currently filtered results with their source, evidence, search time and any warnings. No Reddit sign-in is needed. Please use public information responsibly.
